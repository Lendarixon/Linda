for teams, schools and companies

Checks that can be filed, compared and repeated — without sending a single text to anyone.

Batch check and CSV export

Drop any number of PDF, DOCX, TXT or MD files: they are checked one after another, with CSV export for your records.

Document comparison

Version comparison and AI check in one window: the program puts two documents side by side (shows identical paragraphs and verbatim matches), and gives an independent verdict: whether the text was written by a human, generated by AI, or contains mixed edits.

History that stays with you

Every check is saved with its text and result, only on your computer: folders, search, sorting, bulk delete, automatic clean-up after 30, 90 or 365 days. Switch it off any time.

Organization licence

One site, 200 devices, updates for the whole licence year (including new models), invoice with VAT details. After one-time activation all keys work fully offline, with no internet connection.
