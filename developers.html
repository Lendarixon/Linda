06 — licensing

free or commercial

One checking algorithm: as a simple Windows app or a console tool for developers. The licensing rules are the same.

Free: personal, non-commercial

The app, the command-line tools and the neural networks (models) are free for personal non-commercial use (CC BY-NC 4.0): your own texts, study, hobby, non-commercial research. No registration, no key, no limits.

Commercial licence

For paid work, clients, companies, schools and universities, or products. One licence covers everything together: the app, the command-line tools and the models. You buy the right to use the software commercially, not the models separately and not a subscription service.

07 — for developers

command line and models

Prefer the terminal or Python? The same detector runs from the command line — under the same rules as the app.

The linda_pro package and tools (python -m linda_pro) live on GitHub; the models and calibration (about 2.9 GB) live on Hugging Face and the app downloads them for you. Personal non-commercial use is free (CC BY-NC 4.0); commercial use needs the same licence key as the app: the key is your proof of purchase, the tools themselves never ask for it.

What do I need?

Minimum: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 8 GB of RAM and 4 GB of disk space. No dedicated graphics card required: a standard 1,000-word text (about 2–3 pages) checks in a couple of seconds on a modern PC and in 10–20 seconds on an ordinary office computer.
Recommended: 16 GB of memory and a modern graphics card (speeds the check up to a fraction of a second). The app picks the optimal mode by itself.

Details for tech-savvy readers

Minimum processor: 4 cores (measured 4 s per 1,000 words on an 8-core Ryzen 7 7800X3D); recommended 6 or more cores, 16 GB of memory, an SSD. Any DirectX 12 graphics card works through DirectML — AMD Radeon RX, NVIDIA GeForce RTX/GTX or Intel Arc, no CUDA or ROCm installation needed. The app uses a separate (discrete) card automatically; on first use it prepares the graphics version of the models once (about a minute). Measured on an AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT (16 GB): 0.3 s per 1,000 words; other cards should work but were not tested. The models need about 3 GB of video memory; loading the models at start takes 5–10 s.
