# Model card — Linda-Pro 1.3.1

Intended use: screening English, Polish and Russian texts for AI generation, showing per-window scores; human review required. Not for sole evidence of misconduct.
Architecture: Stylo-D (LightGBM + n-grams, CPU), Linda-Essay-M (DeBERTa-v3-large, weight interpolation of three fine-tuned checkpoints D 0.25 / H 0.25 / I 0.5) and Linda-Multi-M (mDeBERTa-v3-base, same recipe); windowed scoring (~300 words, mean of the top 25% of window margins); one frozen decision rule per language (English: weights 0.8 / 0 / 0.2; Polish and Russian: equal-weight mean of the three z-scores), thresholds frozen on development human texts (`calibration/routing.json`).
Training: continued from Linda-Essay v3 / Linda-Multi v2 on open-model AI texts, rewrites by open models, real outputs of commercial humanizers from public CC BY 4.0 benchmarks, human texts (web text dated before 2022, scientific abstracts, public-domain fiction, learner essays from ASAP 2.0 and IELTS sets). Version 1.3.1 contains no text generated by Llama 3.x models; see DATA_BOM.md.
Evaluation: EVIDENCE_PACK.md (section 9) and the technical report. Known limits: humanizers evolve monthly; old short texts from small open models are caught only about 47% of the time; non-native exam essays (TOEFL 5.5% false flags in sensitive mode); MAGE human texts 2.0% false flags; rewrites by another model are caught about 56-59% of the time (Polish, Russian); Polish and Russian sets are small.
