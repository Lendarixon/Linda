What this means

On this historical set, Assay detected about 81 out of every 100 rewritten AI texts; GPTZero detected about 48. Higher means more AI texts found here. It does not tell us how often human writing is wrongly flagged.

Method and limitations

Thresholds and human-text false alarms were not matched. These historical competitor versions are not a live ranking. The latest October cycle has changed; it must not be mixed into this chart. What this means

In the newer test, Trace 1.3.0 selected the right reference class in 71 of 90 cases, Burrows’ Delta in 64 and Writeprints-style in 60. The tasks combine student authors with science/news topic groups. This is not 78.9% accuracy at identifying a person.

Method and limitations

23 Oct 2026 report: the same 90 queries across four domains, about 750 words per text. Comparison methods were reproduced locally. Authors, disciplines and news desks are different class types. All domains, additional lengths and harder tasks appear below. Embedded authorship_engine.py has the same SHA-256 as the audited project source; a complete installed-app run was not repeated here.

FAQ

A few useful answers.

Do I need both apps?+

Only if you need both workflows. Detector works with text; Probe searches your files. Install either one independently.

Is the Humanizer a separate subscription?+

It is part of Linda Detector and requires a paid Linda Detector licence. Personal and Team are one-time licences; Organization is annual.

Do documents leave my computer?+

Desktop processing runs locally. Downloads, updates and licence validation use the network. Text entered into the online demo is processed on a server.

Can Trace prove who wrote a text?+

No. It compares supplied texts and ranks style similarity. Genre, length, editing and shared sources can change the result.
