03 — why trust it

honest numbers, your texts stay yours

Nothing here is invented for marketing: every figure below comes from the results section and the open technical report.

Your texts never leave your computer

The analysis runs on your PC and, after the first launch, works offline. The internet is needed only for model files, updates and licence-key checks.

Open data and code

Data, tests and limitations are described in the technical report, and the code is on GitHub. You can check everything yourself.

We show the weak spots too

The drop on short MAGE texts (−6.0 pts) and on StealthGPT (−2.5 pts) is published above. No detector is perfect: false alarms are 0–2%.

Clear payment and refunds

Payments go through Polar. Personal and Team keys: full refund within 14 days, no questions asked.

History stays on your computer

Checks, folders and texts are stored only on your computer, with automatic clean-up. The free version needs no key at all; a paid key, once activated, also works without internet.

Russian and Polish: what changed in 1.3

Russian and Polish now run on the same models as English. On our held-out test sets (350 AI + 350 human texts per language) the verdict “AI” catches about 80% (Polish) and 78% (Russian) of AI texts at 0.9% and 0.6% false alarms (version 1.1: 39% and 57%). Texts rewritten by another model are caught less often (Gemini-rewritten: Polish 48%, Russian 50%). The sets are small, so expect ±5 points. We did not compare Russian or Polish against other detectors.
