Profile

A political scientist who writes and builds.

Third-year political science student at the University of Warsaw and a winner of international essay competitions on regulation, institutions and artificial intelligence. My essays are published in Poland and abroad; I work where economics, law and technology meet — dispersed knowledge, the information content of prices, and the rules autonomous agents will need.

Alongside essays I build software: Linda, a local detector of AI-generated text, and Linda Probe, a local document search tool with OCR. Both run entirely on the user's computer.

Projects

Tools I build and ship.

Linda

A local detector of AI-generated text for English, Russian and Polish. Transformer models combined with stylometric features, packaged as a Windows application: the text never leaves the machine.

github.com/Lendarixon/Linda

Linda Probe

Local document search for Windows with OCR and semantic search. Documents are indexed and searched only on the user's computer.

github.com/Lendarixon/Linda-Probe

Also inside Linda Detector

Humanizer rewrites stiff, templated sentences and shows every change next to the original. Trace compares writing styles and ranks how similar texts are — a clue for review, not proof of authorship.
