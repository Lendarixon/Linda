Privacy

Your texts

The application analyses texts on your computer. They are not uploaded and the author does not receive them.

What the application sends

  • Model download and update check: a request to Hugging Face for model files and a small signed manifest (Hugging Face sees your IP address, as any web server does).
  • Application update check: the same, and a download from GitHub if you accept an update.
  • Licence key: when you enter a key, the key and a random device label (“Windows-xxxxxx”, not your computer name) are sent to Polar to activate it, and re-checked about once a week.

Purchases

Payments, receipts and the licence key are handled by Polar as Merchant of Record under its own privacy policy. The author receives your name, e-mail and country as the buyer of a key, to provide support, and nothing is sold or shared.
