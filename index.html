About Linda Probe↗

What the result actually says

A score, not a verdict

The percentage is how strongly the model leans towards machine-written prose. 60–80% means "worth a closer look", not "guilty".

The exact sentences

Every flagged sentence is highlighted in your text, so you can judge each one yourself instead of trusting a single number.

What it looks at

Uniform sentence length, low burstiness, stock connectives ("furthermore", "it is important to note"), and a flat rhythm of clause lengths.

What it cannot do

It does not know who wrote the text, cannot prove misconduct, and gets weaker on short passages, poetry, dialogue and heavy editing.

Where it is honest about limits

Short texts under 20 words, lists, code and tables are not what this check is built for. Non-native writing and heavily edited machine text can score high; a human editor can score low. Use the result as a reason to look, never as a decision on its own.

Three languages, one model

English, Polish and Russian are supported with the same detector. Other languages fall back to English scoring and are less reliable — the app says so in the result instead of guessing.

FAQ

A few useful answers.

Do I need both apps?+

Only if you need both workflows. Detector works with text; Probe searches your files. Install either one independently.

Is the Humanizer a separate subscription?+

It is part of Linda Detector and requires a paid Linda Detector licence. Personal and Team are one-time licences; Organization is annual. Humanizer edits style: it removes stiff, templated phrasing. It is not built to hide AI use.

Do documents leave my computer?+

Desktop processing runs locally. Downloads, updates and licence validation use the network. Text entered into the online demo is processed on a server.

Can Trace prove who wrote a text?+

No. Trace compares your texts and ranks how similar their style is. Genre, length, editing and shared sources all change the result, so it is a clue, not proof.
